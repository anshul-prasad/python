A pen is a common writing instrument used to apply ink to a surface, typically paper, for the purpose of writing or drawing. It consists of a tip or nib that deposits ink as it moves across the writing surface. Pens come in various types, including ballpoint, fountain, gel, and rollerball, each offering different writing experiences and ink properties. The invention of the pen has been pivotal in the development of written communication, enabling the recording and sharing of information throughout history. Today, pens remain essential tools in everyday life, valued for their convenience, portability, and ability to produce precise and lasting marks.
